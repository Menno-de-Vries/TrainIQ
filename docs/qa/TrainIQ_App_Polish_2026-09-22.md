[Service foreground removal/detachment](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/app/Service) and [Android 16 notification handling](https://android.googlesource.com/platform/frameworks/base/+/refs/heads/android16-release/services/core/java/com/android/server/notification/NotificationManagerService.java) establish that foreground notification ownership/flag updates are separate platform work. A distinct countdown key avoids that ownership collision; the exact platform interleaving in the failed runs was not traced.

Pinned Compose BOM 2024.12.01, Navigation 2.8.7, Room 2.7.0 and existing Kotlin/AGP configuration remain unchanged. Benchmark observations above are public product patterns, not claims about competitors' internal architecture. No visual design was copied.
